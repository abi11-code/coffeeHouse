About us

A little corner of calm in a busy day

Brew & Bean started with a simple idea: great coffee should feel personal. We roast in small batches, pour with care, and treat every guest like a regular.

Whether you need a quick espresso on the go or a long afternoon with a book, there is always a seat and a warm cup waiting for you.
